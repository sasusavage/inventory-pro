Everything You Need

All Features. One Platform.

Every tool your shop needs — POS, inventory, staff, customers, reports and more. Each plan unlocks the right set of features for your business size.

🖥️

Point of Sale

Fast checkout with barcode scanning, product search, customer lookup, hold & resume transactions, and SMS receipts.

📶

Offline Mode

Keep selling even without internet. Installs as a PWA on any device — phone, tablet, or desktop.

📦

Inventory Management

Track every product — stock levels, low-stock alerts, reorder thresholds, product variants (size/colour), and full movement history.

📋

Purchase Orders

Create POs, receive stock (full or partial), track outstanding orders and auto-update inventory on receipt.

👥

Customer Management

Customer profiles, full purchase history, outstanding balances, loyalty points and printable account statements.

🎁

Loyalty Points

Earn points on every purchase. Redeem at checkout. Configure earn and redemption rates from your settings.

🛡️

Staff & Roles

Owner, Manager, and Cashier roles with fine-grained permissions. Per-cashier sales tracking and manager overrides.

📈

P&L Reports

Revenue, COGS, gross profit, operating expenses and net profit — all in one clean report with date filters.

🤖

AI Analytics Pro

AI-generated insights on sales trends, top products, and inventory health. Delivered on demand or by daily Telegram report.

🔄

Branch Stock Transfers Pro

Move stock between your branches with a full approval flow and automatic stock deduction on completion.

💬

SMS Receipts Pro

Automatically send an SMS receipt to every customer after a sale using Africa's Talking — fully configurable.

🌙

End-of-Day Report Pro

Daily cash reconciliation — expected vs actual cash count, payment method breakdown, and top products sold.

💸

Expense Tracking

Log all operating expenses by category — rent, salary, transport, utilities — and see them on your P&L.

🚚

Supplier Management

Supplier directory, payment history, and smart reorder suggestions with one-click draft POs.

🏆

Top Customers

Leaderboard of your best customers by spend. See outstanding balances and print account statements.

📱

Install on Any Device

Works as a PWA — install on Android, iPhone, Windows, or Mac. No app store. One codebase, every platform.
