Every tool your shop needs — POS, inventory, staff, customers, reports and more. Turn on only what you need.

Point of Sale

Fast checkout with barcode scanning, product search, customer lookup, discounts per item or whole order, and receipt printing.

📶

Offline Mode

Keep selling even without internet. Sales queue automatically and sync to the cloud when you're back online.

📦

Inventory Management

Track every product — stock levels, low-stock alerts, reorder thresholds, and full movement history.

📋

Purchase Orders

Create POs, receive stock against them (full or partial), track outstanding orders and auto-update inventory on receipt.

Customer Management

Customer profiles, full purchase history, outstanding balances, loyalty points and printable account statements.

🎁

Loyalty Points

Earn points on every purchase. Redeem at checkout. Configure earn and redemption rates from your settings.

Staff & Roles

Owner, Manager, and Cashier roles. Per-cashier sales tracking, manager overrides for refunds and discounts.

📈

P&L Reports

Revenue, COGS, gross profit, operating expenses and net profit — all in one clean report with date filters.

🏆

Top Customers

Leaderboard of your best customers by spend. See outstanding balances and link straight to their statement.

💸

Expense Tracking

Log all operating expenses by category — rent, salary, transport, utilities — and see them on your P&L.

Refunds

Process refunds with approval flow, stock auto-restore, and full audit trail on every returned item.

AI Analytics

AI-generated insights on your sales trends, top products, and inventory health delivered on demand or by scheduled Telegram report.

Stock Adjustments
